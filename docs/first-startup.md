# First Startup

## Overview

This guide explains how to safely power on the Eco Robot ER-10 for the first time and verify that the system is operating correctly.

Complete the Installation Guide before performing this procedure.

---

## Before You Begin

Verify that:

- Installation is complete.
- The robot is securely mounted.
- Emergency Stop buttons are released.
- The work area is clear.
- The controller is connected to power.

---

## Step 1 – Power On the Controller

1. Turn the controller power switch to **ON**.
2. Wait for the controller to complete its startup sequence.
3. Verify that the Status LED changes to solid green.

---

## Step 2 – Log In

1. Open the Eco Robot Controller in your web browser.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. Select **Log In**.

---

## Step 3 – Connect to the Robot

1. Select the connected robot from the dashboard.
2. Confirm that the robot status displays **Ready**.
3. Verify that no active alarms are present.

---

## Step 4 – Home the Robot

1. Select **Maintenance**.
2. Click **Home Robot**.
3. Wait until the robot reaches its home position.

---

!!! warning "Robot Motion"

    Keep clear of the robot while the homing sequence is in progress.

---

## Step 5 – Verify System Status

Confirm the following:

| Check | Expected Result |
|--------|-----------------|
| Controller Status | Online |
| Robot Status | Ready |
| Network Status | Connected |
| Safety Status | Normal |

---

## Startup Complete

The Eco Robot ER-10 is now ready for normal operation.

Continue to the **Robot Operation** guide.
