# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I start the robot?

Follow the procedure in the **First Startup** guide.

---

## What should I do if the Emergency Stop is pressed?

Release the Emergency Stop, verify the area is safe, and restart the system.

---

## How often should maintenance be performed?

Daily inspections are recommended. Weekly and monthly maintenance should follow the Maintenance schedule.

---

## Can I operate the robot without safety guards?

No. Safety guards must always be installed during operation.

---

## Where can I find technical specifications?

See the **Technical Specifications** section of this documentation.
