# Safety Instructions

## Overview

This section provides important safety information for the safe installation, operation, and maintenance of the Eco Robot ER-10.

Failure to follow these instructions may result in equipment damage, personal injury, or system malfunction.

---

## General Safety Rules

Always follow these basic safety rules before operating the robot:

- Read all documentation before operating the robot.
- Wear the required personal protective equipment (PPE).
- Keep unauthorized personnel outside the robot work cell.
- Never bypass safety devices or interlocks.
- Ensure the emergency stop button is easily accessible.

---


## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

Operators and maintenance personnel should wear:

- Safety glasses
- Safety shoes
- Protective gloves (when performing maintenance)

---

!!! warning "Moving Robot Hazard"

    The robot arm may move without warning during automatic operation. Never enter the robot work cell while the robot is in Automatic mode.

---

!!! caution "Pinch Points"

    Keep hands and fingers away from joints and moving mechanical parts while the robot is powered on.

---

!!! note "Emergency Stop"

    Before performing maintenance, press the Emergency Stop button and verify that all robot motion has stopped.

---

## Before Starting the Robot

Verify that:

- The work area is clear of obstacles.
- All safety guards are installed.
- Emergency stop buttons are functioning correctly.
- No personnel are inside the robot cell.
- The robot is free from visible damage.

---

---
## Safe Shutdown Procedure

1. Stop the current robot program.
2. Return the robot to its home position.
3. Press the **Emergency Stop** if required.
4. Turn the controller power switch to **OFF**.
5. Disconnect power before performing maintenance.
